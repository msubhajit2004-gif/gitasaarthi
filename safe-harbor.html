5-4-3-2-1 grounding, for acute anxiety

  1. 5 things you see. Notice five colours, objects or patterns around you.
  2. 4 things you feel. Your clothing, the floor under your feet, your hands together.
  3. 3 sounds you hear. Distant traffic, a fan, your own breath.
  4. 2 scents you smell. Coffee, rain, or simply cool air.
  5. 1 truth to say aloud. “I am safe in this moment. My breath is steady.”

GitaSaarthi is a space for reflection, not medical care, diagnosis or emergency help.
