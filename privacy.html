Privacy

GitaSaarthi keeps as little about you as it can. This page says what that is, who can see it, and how to take it back.

What we store

Your name, your email, and a hash of your password (never the password itself). Your practice days, your mood check-ins, your journal, and the last topic you looked at. That is all.

Conversations and crisis events

When you talk with the guide, or when the app shows you crisis help, we record the topic only, such as "anxiety". We never store the text of what you wrote. Admins can see these events together with your name and email, so that a person can follow up with you if that is needed.

Gemini is optional

The guide can use Google's Gemini if you switch it on. When it is on, your messages are sent to Google to get a reply. Your Gemini key is stored encrypted on this server with your account, so you do not have to enter it again. When Gemini is on, your messages pass through this server to Google and nowhere else. If you leave it off, nothing goes to Google. You can remove the key at any time in Settings, and deleting your account removes it too.

Your data, your control

To export everything we hold, open Settings, then Account, then Download my data. In the same place you can change your password, sign out your other devices, and delete your account. Deleting is permanent: your account, journal, check-ins and events are removed and cannot be brought back.

What we do not do

No ads. No tracking. We do not sell or share your data.

Not a medical service

GitaSaarthi is a space for reflection, not medical care or emergency help. If you are in crisis, the helplines on Safe Harbor answer at any hour.
